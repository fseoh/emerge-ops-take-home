# Outreach drafts

Templates for the two chat-invite texts in `COMMUNITY_PLAN.md` ("What it is"). **Nothing here is sent.** They are drafts for review.

| File | When | Language |
|---|---|---|
| `touch1_en.txt`, `touch1_es.txt` | First touch: days 0–2 after first video | English, Spanish |
| `touch2_en.txt`, `touch2_es.txt` | Second touch: days 4–5, only if still not in the chat | English, Spanish |
| `touch1_ht.txt`, `touch2_ht.txt` | Same | Haitian Creole: **NEEDS TRANSLATION** |

**How to use them**

- Send only to students in the **treatment** group, from the day's list on the dashboard ("Who to reach today").
- Send in the student's `plan_study_time` window, on a `plan_study_days` day.
- Use the student's `preferred_language`. Haitian Creole stays unsent until a fluent reviewer translates it.
- Fill the placeholders: `[sender first name]` (the real coach or community lead), `[city]`, `[WhatsApp link]` (that city's group), `[next lesson]` (the student's next lesson number). Don't add other personal details.
- Log every send in the outreach log (group, send time, touch number, reply, STOP).

**Checks each draft passes** (CLAUDE.md, "Writing for and about our students")

- Under 320 characters with the longest realistic fill (name "Christopher", city "Sacramento", a full WhatsApp link). Longest: 301 characters (touch1_es.txt).
- No mention of a record, parole, probation or conviction. No deadlines, urgency, or job or pay promises.
- No guilt ("you fell behind", "you haven't"). One next step: join the group.
- Includes "Reply STOP to stop texts" (in Spanish, "Responde STOP…"; STOP stays in English because carriers act on that keyword).
